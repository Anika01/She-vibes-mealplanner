# She-vibes-mealplanner
Every morning starts with the same question: "What should we cook today?" In my home, meal planning means balancing the nutritional needs of a toddler, senior citizens, my husband, and myself—all while managing available ingredients, reducing waste, and staying realistic about cooking time. Rasoi Copilot is an AI-powered meal planning assistant that removes this daily decision burden.
 Why I Built This
Meal planning isn't just about food—it's about cognitive load.
In a multi-generational household, a single meal decision requires balancing:
* Nutrition
* Preferences
* Age-specific requirements
* Available ingredients
* Cooking effort
* Food waste
I built Rasoi Copilot to explore how AI can reduce invisible household labor and help families make better food decisions with less mental effort.
 Primary Users
Me (Household Planner)
Needs:
* Quick daily decisions
* Balanced nutrition
* Less mental load
Household Cook
Needs:
* Clear instructions
* No daily planning discussions
* Practical meals
Family Members
Needs:
* Appropriate nutrition
* Familiar vegetarian food
* Variety without complexity

 Current Limitations
* Supports vegetarian households only
* Pantry tracking is manually maintained
* Designed for a single household
* Nutrition recommendations are guidance, not medical advice
 Long-Term Vision
Today, Rasoi Copilot plans meals.
Tomorrow, it could:
* Generate grocery lists automatically
* Track pantry inventory
* Predict ingredient shortages
* Adapt plans based on family feedback
* Coordinate nutrition across generations
The goal is to become a trusted household decision-making assistant that quietly handles food planning in the background.
